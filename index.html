Engineering Reference

Project Blueprint & System Architecture

Derived from the comprehensive Seva First specification for the rural Maharashtra livestock platform.

Modular Architecture & Technology Stack

A resilient, mobile-first design ready for rural low-connectivity conditions with clear separation between user interfaces, core workflows, and inference APIs.

Layer Recommended Implementation Role in PashuSachet
Frontend Next.js / HTML5 + TypeScript, Responsive UI Mobile-first farmer app, vet triage portal, officer dashboards in Marathi/Hindi/English.
Backend API FastAPI (Python) Modular Monolith Authentication, case lifecycle, appointment dispatch, and clinical timelines.
Database PostgreSQL 16 + PostGIS extension Relational animal health records, spatial queries, and taluka cluster tracking.
Machine Learning scikit-learn, XGBoost, CatBoost Species-aware disease classifier, spatio-temporal risk, baseline anomaly detection.
GIS Mapping Leaflet / MapLibre & GeoJSON District/taluka-level heatmaps, disease clusters, and livestock density layers.

The Three Machine Learning Capabilities

Model A: Disease Classification

Purpose: Rank plausible disease categories from structured symptoms & animal context.

Inputs: Species, breed, age, rectal temperature, appetite, lesions, lameness, milk drop.

Baseline: Logistic Regression, Random Forest, CatBoost

Model B: Outbreak Risk (GIS)

Purpose: Estimate future disease risk for an area & horizon using historical patterns & climate.

Inputs: Season, lagged period counts, spatial neighbors, rainfall, temperature, humidity.

Benchmark: Seasonal-naive & Spatio-temporal Trees

Model C: Anomaly Detection

Purpose: Flag vital measurements unusual relative to an individual animal's baseline.

Inputs: Daily milk yield, rumination cycles, pedometer activity, core temperature.

Methods: Rolling robust stats, Isolation Forest, LOF

Social Safeguards, Ethics & AI Boundaries

Social Safeguards

  • Do not exclude farmers without smartphones; support assisted village kiosk access.
  • Do not expose farm-level reports publicly in ways that stigmatize villages or affect milk procurement.
  • Keep essential health information and emergency triage completely free to farmers.

Clinical & AI Boundaries

  • Preliminary decision support only; never autonomous prescription or diagnosis.
  • Urgent warning rules must immediately prompt emergency care without waiting for model run.
  • Human confirmation required: certified veterinarians confirm all final diagnoses and medications.
